# The Pakled is a helmet holder

The bot is an eligible member of its own helmet system and is guaranteed a helmet in every
ceremony, chosen at random like anyone else's. The central gag requires it: the Pakled inspects the
helmet it received, concludes it is probably not the one it lost, and eventually starts another
ceremony. A bot that never wears a helmet has nothing to inspect and the loop has no engine.

## Consequences

Supersedes the project specification's §5, which excludes bot accounts from the participant
population. One of the ten helmets is spoken for before any human is considered, and the Pakled can
draw The Biggest Helmet — in which case it stays suspicious anyway, per §11.
